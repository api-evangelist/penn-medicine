Penn Medicine researchers Hongjun Song and Guo-li Ming have developed miniaturized brain organoid models that can serve as avatars for testing cancer treatment, enabling scientists to test hundreds of potential therapies simultaneously on patient-derived tumor samples. Their work advances brain research through cross-disciplinary partnerships.
